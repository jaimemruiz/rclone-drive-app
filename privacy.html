Privacy policy

This application is a personal OAuth client used only by its owner to access their own Google Drive accounts with rclone on their own computer.

  • Data from Google Drive is transferred only between Google Drive and the owner's own computer.
  • No data is collected, stored on third-party servers, shared, sold or used for advertising.
  • Access tokens are stored only on the owner's computer.
  • The application is not offered to other users.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact: github.com/jaimemruiz

Política de privacidad

Esta aplicación es un cliente OAuth personal que solo usa su propietario para acceder a sus propias cuentas de Google Drive con rclone desde su propio ordenador. No recoge, comparte ni vende datos; los tokens de acceso se guardan solo en su ordenador.
